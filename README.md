##  About the Game

It's 4:45 AM. You're working the night shift, and all you want to do is leave the office.

But when the clock hits 5:00 AM, time resets. Your collected items disappear, and you're back at the beginning.

The only thing you keep is what you've learned.

Explore the office, solve puzzles, collect keycards, and figure out how to escape before time runs out.

##  Features

- **Time loop mechanic:** The game resets when the clock reaches 5:00 AM.
- **Knowledge persists:** Items are lost when time resets, but your understanding of the puzzles remains.
- **Office exploration:** Explore multiple connected areas and unlock new rooms.
- **Puzzle-solving:** Discover clues, solve riddles, and enter access codes.
- **Keycard progression:** Find the keycards needed to progress and reach the exit.
- **NPC interactions:** Keep your distance from nearby colleagues to interact with objects safely.

##  Puzzle Progression

- **Level 1–2:** Explore the office and work your way through the initial areas.
- **Level 3:** Find the normal Keycard and discover the supervisor computer's code to obtain the Supervisor A20 Keycard.
- **Level 4:** Solve the clock riddle to reveal the code, then use the terminal to obtain the Master Keycard.
- **Escape:** Use the normal Keycard and Master Keycard to unlock the exit.

The Supervisor A20 Keycard is needed to reach Level 4.

##  The Time Loop

1. Start at 4:45 AM.
2. Explore, collect items, and solve puzzles.
3. Reach 5:00 AM and the loop resets.
4. Lose your collected items, but retain your knowledge.
5. Use what you've learned to make progress on your next attempt.

A wall clock can also rewind time.
